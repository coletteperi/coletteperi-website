Dorell Fabrics

An integrated, multi-platform campaign launching a new line of performance fabrics. A one-person production, from concept and script through the shoot and final hero assets, blending live action and stop-motion to turn life's messiest moments into proof. Coffee, spaghetti, and red wine all meet their match.

Client
Dorell Fabrics
Role
Creative Director & Director: a full one-person production
Channels
Integrated across social, paid, web & brand channels
Deliverables
Hero video assets: Coffee, Spaghetti, Wine
Craft
Concept, scriptwriting, casting, wardrobe, direction, stop-motion, edit

The Brief

Make “stain-proof” believable, memorable, and fun to watch for a new line of performance fabrics, in a campaign that works everywhere the brand shows up.

The Big Idea

Lean into the mess: each film stages a real-life disaster on the fabric, then lets the product win in one satisfying, handcrafted beat.

Visual Craft

Live action grounds the story and stop-motion delivers the moment the spill doesn’t stick, with one art direction system tying the three films together.

Concept to Delivery

A full one-person production: concept, script, casting, wardrobe, direction, and the final hero assets.

Built to Scale

One visual system adapted across formats and platforms, with a repeatable mess → moment → magic structure for any new stain.
