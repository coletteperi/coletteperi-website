Ruggable

A social-first launch campaign for Ruggable's washable jute rugs, using handcrafted stop-motion to turn a simple product truth into scroll-stopping, shareable stories.

The Brief

Launch a new product (a jute rug you can actually wash) to an audience that already loves the look of jute but has accepted that it can’t be cleaned. The work needed to land that news fast, in the feed.

The Big Idea

Home life, in miniature. Handcrafted rooms where everyday chaos (spills, crumbs, muddy paws) plays out on the rug, then the rug simply washes clean. A familiar home moment becomes a delightful, ownable demonstration of the product benefit.

Visual Craft

I designed and built a cohesive set of interiors with a consistent palette and styling that stays true to Ruggable’s aesthetic, so each scene feels like a real, aspirational home while the stop-motion keeps it playful and unexpected.

Integrated Execution

  • Modular storytelling: individual room scenes like the Kitchen work as standalone social posts and build into one full hero film.
  • Launch-ready system: one visual world flexible enough for paid, organic, and future rooms or products.
  • Concept to delivery: led the idea, set design, animation, and final edit.
