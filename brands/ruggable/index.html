Ruggable

A social-first launch campaign for Ruggable's washable jute rugs. Shot on a real kitchen set, with a stop-motion smoothie spill that turns a simple product truth into a scroll-stopping, shareable story.

Client
Ruggable
Role
Creative Lead & Art Director
Channels
Paid & organic social
Deliverables
Hero launch film, social cutdowns
Craft
Concept, art direction, set styling, stop-motion, edit

The Brief

Launch a washable jute rug to an audience that loves the look of jute but assumes it can’t be cleaned, and land that news fast in the feed.

The Big Idea

Make the mess the message: in a styled kitchen, a stop-motion smoothie spill lands on the jute rug, then the rug simply washes clean.

Visual Craft

A real, aspirational kitchen set grounds the story, and the handcrafted stop-motion spill is the unexpected beat that stops the scroll.

Integrated Execution

A modular kitchen scene that works as a standalone social post and builds into the full launch film, led from idea and set styling through animation and final edit.
