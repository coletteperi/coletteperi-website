Pampers

Gentle, joyful stop-motion for Pampers, born from an in-person creative briefing and brainstorm with the Pampers marketing team at their Ohio headquarters, and produced on location for Amazon and social.

Client
Pampers
Role
Creative Lead & Producer, brainstorm through final delivery
Channels
Amazon product pages, Amazon ads, social media
Deliverables
Two video assets: “Unbox” and “Moms Texting Moms”
Craft
Concepting, art direction, on-location production, stop-motion, edit

The Brief

Bring Pampers’ key product messaging to life in a way that feels warm, clear, and instantly understood, on social and on Amazon product pages.

In the Room

A full day at Pampers’ Ohio headquarters for an in-person creative briefing and brainstorm with their marketing team, pressure-testing ideas against the brand’s key messages.

From Ideas to Execution

Produced on location: art directed and animated two video assets that carry the messaging through simple, relatable moments.

Where It Lives

Both assets performed well across Pampers’ social and Amazon channels, including product pages and ads, in one consistent visual world.
