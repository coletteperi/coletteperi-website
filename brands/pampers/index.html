Pampers

Gentle, joyful stop-motion for Pampers, born from a full day of in-person brainstorming with the Pampers creative team at their Ohio headquarters and built to live on Amazon and across social.

The Brief

Pampers needed fresh creative to bring its key product messaging to life in a way that feels warm, clear, and instantly understood, whether a parent is scrolling social or comparing options on an Amazon product page.

In the Room

The Pampers team flew me out to their headquarters in Ohio for a full day in the office with their creative team. Together we brainstormed campaign ideas, pressure-tested concepts against the brand’s key messages, and shaped the directions with the most potential to connect with parents.

From Ideas to Execution

I flew home and brought the chosen ideas to life in my studio, art directing and animating each piece by hand. The result is a set of gentle, joyful stop-motion videos that carry the brand’s messaging through simple, relatable moments.

Where It Lives

  • Amazon product pages: videos that help explain the product right where parents make their purchase decision.
  • Amazon ads: short, clear, scroll-stopping assets built for retail media placements.
  • Social media: the same visual world adapted for social feeds, keeping the brand consistent at every touchpoint.
