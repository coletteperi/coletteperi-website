Lucky Brand

A stop-motion paid social campaign launching the Lucky Brand Rewards Credit Card, told through the brand’s own clothing, denim, tags, and holiday-season props.

Client
Lucky Brand
Role
Concept, styling, art direction, animation, edit
Channels
Paid social
Deliverables
Three stop-motion paid social videos and a holiday gift guide video

The Brief

Launch a new rewards credit card in a way that feels like Lucky Brand, not a bank, and make the card’s savings easy to understand in a few seconds of scrolling.

The Big Idea

Let the product world tell the story. Denim, leather patches, hang tags, and the brand’s signature clover print set the scene, then the card appears among them like one more piece of the Lucky Brand wardrobe.

Visual Craft

Handcrafted stop-motion on a clean flat-lay, with a seasonal, giftable feel. The offer itself is animated by hand: crumpled paper notes unfold to reveal 15% off the first card purchase, 5% off every purchase after, or the option to donate that 5% to Communities In Schools.

Built for Paid Social

Three short, standalone stop-motion videos made for paid social, each one quick to read, on brand, and focused on a single message about the card. Colette also made a holiday gift guide video as part of the campaign.
