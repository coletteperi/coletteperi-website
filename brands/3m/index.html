3M

An integrated holiday campaign for 3M Scotch Flex & Seal: one handcrafted idea built to live on broadcast TV and scale across social, with a single visual world tying every touchpoint together.

Client
3M · Scotch Flex & Seal
Role
Creative Lead & Art Director, concept through final delivery
Channels
Broadcast TV, paid & organic social
Deliverables
:30 TV spot, social-first cutdowns
Craft
Set design, prop fabrication, stop-motion, lighting, edit

The Brief

Make a practical product feel holiday-ready, with one idea strong enough for national TV and for stopping the scroll on social.

The Big Idea

Holiday wrapping, reimagined as pure play: the product seals, wraps, and protects in a handmade world that feels warm, giftable, and seasonal.

Visual Craft

A tight holiday palette, tactile handmade textures, and clean product-hero framing, art directed and animated by hand.

Integrated Execution

Planned TV-first and social-native from day one, with one shared world across every touchpoint, led from ideation through final edit.
