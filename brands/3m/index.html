3M

An integrated holiday campaign for 3M Scotch Flex & Seal: one handcrafted idea built to live on broadcast TV and scale across social, with a single visual world tying every touchpoint together.

The Brief

Make a practical, functional product feel like part of the holiday season, and give the brand one idea strong enough to anchor a national TV moment while still stopping the scroll on social.

The Big Idea

Holiday wrapping, reimagined as pure play. Instead of telling viewers what Flex & Seal does, the campaign shows it, letting the product seal, wrap, and protect in a handmade world that feels warm, giftable, and unmistakably seasonal.

Visual Craft

I built a cohesive art direction system (a tight holiday palette, tactile handmade textures, and clean product-hero framing) so the brand reads instantly in a :30 broadcast spot and in a vertical social feed alike. Every frame was art directed and animated by hand for a crafted, premium finish.

Integrated Execution

  • TV first, social native: the :30 broadcast spot was planned alongside social assets from day one, not cut down as an afterthought.
  • One world, every touchpoint: shared palette, sets, and motion language kept the campaign consistent across channels.
  • Concept to delivery: led the work from ideation and boards through production, animation, and final edit.
